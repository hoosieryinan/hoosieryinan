# Templates for Max Business Builder

## Universal decision brief
```markdown
# Decision Brief: [Topic]

## Situation summary
[What happened / current state]

## Key issue
[The decision, risk, or blocker]

## Options
| Option | Pros | Cons | Risk level | Effort | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Option A | [Pros] | [Cons] | Low/Med/High | Low/Med/High | Yes/No |

## Recommendation
[Recommended option and reason]

## Risks
| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Next actions
| Action | Owner | Due date | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
```

## Meeting preparation template
```markdown
# Meeting Prep: [Meeting Name]

## Objective
[What must be achieved]

## Attendees and roles
| Person / Group | Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|---|

## Agenda
1. [Topic]
2. [Topic]
3. Decisions and next actions

## Key messages
- [Message 1]
- [Message 2]

## Decisions needed
| Decision | Options | Decision owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|

## Questions to ask
- [Question]
```

## Follow-up email template
```markdown
Subject: Follow-up: [Topic] — Actions and Next Steps

Hi [Name/Team],

Thank you for the discussion today. Below is a short summary and the agreed next steps.

## Summary
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]

## Actions
| Action | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| [Action] | [Owner] | [Date] |

Please let me know if I missed anything.

Best regards,
Max
```
