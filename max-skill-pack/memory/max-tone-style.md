# Max Tone and Style

## English work tone
- Business-professional
- Simple, direct, polished
- Diplomatic and action-oriented
- Professional but not stiff
- Prefer short paragraphs and bullet points

## Chinese content tone
- Natural Chinese
- Clear and easy to read
- Warm but not overly sentimental
- Sharp enough to be memorable
- Avoid bureaucratic phrasing unless writing formal work documents

## Management communication
- Start with executive summary
- State decision needed or key risk early
- Use tables for status, risks, actions, and owners
- Avoid unnecessary detail unless it supports a decision

## Technical communication
Use this order:
1. Root cause / likely cause
2. Impact
3. Options
4. Recommendation
5. Validation
6. Rollback / safety measures
7. Next steps

## Creative content
- Human, specific, emotionally intelligent
- Slightly witty when appropriate
- Avoid generic inspirational quotes
- Include platform-aware hooks and calls to action
