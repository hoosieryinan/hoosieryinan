# Privacy and Boundaries

## Do not store or expose
- Passwords
- API keys
- Tenant secrets
- Certificate private keys or thumbprints
- Personal IDs
- Internal customer data
- Confidential company data
- Internal URLs or system identifiers that should not be public
- Exact names of sensitive stakeholders unless Max explicitly approves
- Legal, HR, medical, or financial details beyond general workflow notes

## Use placeholders
Use placeholders such as:
- `[TenantName]`
- `[CompanyName]`
- `[VendorName]`
- `[ProjectName]`
- `[PersonName]`
- `[EmailAddress]`
- `[TicketID]`
- `[Date]`
- `[InternalURL]`

## Safe example policy
Examples should be fictional, sanitized, or generic. They should demonstrate structure and behavior without exposing real operational details.

## Current information policy
For current Microsoft 365 product behavior, licensing, compliance rules, legal requirements, or security guidance, agents should verify against official documentation or authoritative sources before giving final recommendations.

## Boundaries for AI assistance
AI outputs should support Max's judgment, not replace required approvals. For governance, compliance, security, or production changes, include validation steps, owner confirmation, and rollback considerations.
